    Strategist’s read

    • Rates beat earnings this month. Profits are growing 25–33% in the US and Europe, yet the S&P 500 is set for its worst November since 2008 — a valuation and rate-path story, not a profits story.
    • The AI trade is now a governance and financing question. The Nvidia leadership saga, debt-funded capex worries and chip-export politics hit the most concentrated markets (Korea, Taiwan, Nasdaq-100) hardest.
    • Rotation is the tell. The Dow sits above 50,000 while the Nasdaq-100 flirts with a bear market; value, health care and Indian equities are where money has been hiding.
    • Watch the 9 December Fed decision and Hormuz headlines. A 50 bp hike or a shipping escalation are the two clearest near-term downside catalysts; a deal on the Strait would be the biggest upside surprise for oil, inflation and bonds.
